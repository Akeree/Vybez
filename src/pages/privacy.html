Privacy

Privacy during the preview

Vybez is in early access. Accounts are not open yet, so this site does not collect personal data.

What this site stores

Forms on this site do not send anything anywhere. If you dismiss the preview notice, your browser remembers that choice in local storage on your device. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Before accounts open

We will publish a full privacy policy before any account can be created. It will cover what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how to download or delete it. Imported DJ files are read for track names only and are not stored.

Contact

A contact address for privacy questions will be published with the full policy.

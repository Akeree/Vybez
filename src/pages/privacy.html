Privacy policy

Last updated {{legal:updated}}. {{legal:intro}} This page explains what we collect when you use Vybez, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: {{legal:email}}.

The short version

  • We keep what you give us to run your account and your nights. Nothing else.
  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no selling or renting your data.
  • You can download everything in your account, or delete it, from Settings at any time.

What we collect

Your account. Email address, name, a profile handle, and your password, which we store only as a one-way scrambled hash we can’t read. Anything you add to your profile, such as a city, a bio or links. Your settings and notification choices.

Nights you host on Vybez Social. The details you enter (title, date, time, place, dress code, guest limit), a cover photo if you add one, and the guest list that builds up. Your cover photo is shown to anyone with the invite link, including in link previews on WhatsApp and other apps, so don’t use one you wouldn’t want guests to see. When you take or choose a photo, it is cropped and shrunk on your phone; only that smaller copy is uploaded.

Guests who RSVP. You can RSVP without an account. We keep the name you give, the phone number or email you give (optional), whether you’re bringing a plus-one, any song you request, and your check-in at the door. The host of that night sees these details.

The group (chat, rides and moments). Once the host approves you, you can post messages, offer or join a ride, and share photos in that night’s group. Everyone the host approved, and the host, can see the night chat, the rides list and the photos; a ride’s own chat is seen by the people in that ride and the host. Other guests see your first name and the first letter of your last name, never your phone number or email. Photos are shrunk on your phone before upload. You can remove your own messages, rides and photos, and the host can remove anything.

Check-ins. In the group you can tap “I’m here”, “Heading home” (with roughly when you expect to be home) or “Home safe”. Everyone in that night’s group, and the host, sees your check-in and can send a “checking on you” nudge, which also shows in the night chat. Vybez never reads or shares your location for this: only what you tap. Check-ins are deleted with the night.

Vybez DJ. If you have a Vybez DJ account: the sets, tracklists, crates and live rooms you create, and comments, reactions, saves and follows. When you import a file from DJ software, it is read in your browser for track names, artists, tempo and key; the file itself is never uploaded.

Technical data. Your IP address is used briefly to stop abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts) and is not kept with your account. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you asked for: your account, your nights, invites, RSVPs and guest passes.
  • To send the emails you need, such as a password reset link.
  • To keep Vybez safe: stopping spam, abuse and break-in attempts.
  • To send product news, only if you turn it on in Settings.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we rely on performing our agreement with you for the first two, our legitimate interest in a safe service for the third, and your consent for product news.

Who else handles it

We don’t sell or share your data with advertisers. A few providers run parts of Vybez for us and only process data on our instructions:

  • Vercel hosts the website and its server code.
  • Upstash stores account and night data (servers in the United States).
  • Resend sends account emails such as password resets.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria, including in the United States. They are bound by contract to protect it. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • vz_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It can’t be read by scripts on the page.
  • vz_product remembers whether your account is Vybez Social or Vybez DJ, so the right pages open.
  • Your browser’s local storage keeps small conveniences: a night you started planning, the name you RSVP’d with, and whether you dismissed the early access notice.

We don’t use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it: until you delete your account.
  • A night and its guest list: until the host deletes the night or their account. A guest can leave a guest list from their invite link; their details stay with that night until it is deleted, or until they ask us to remove them.
  • Group messages, rides and photos: until the person who posted them or the host removes them, or the night is deleted. Each chat keeps its latest 500 messages.
  • Password reset links: one hour.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data. Most of this is in Settings: Download my data gives you a copy, and Delete account removes your account, sets, nights and their guest lists straight away. For anything else, or to object to how we use your data, email {{legal:email}} and we’ll reply within 30 days. If you’re not happy with our answer you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored only as a scrambled hash, sign-in cookies are signed and can’t be read by page scripts, every connection is encrypted (HTTPS), and repeated sign-in attempts are slowed down. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we’ll tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell you without delay.

Children

Vybez is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has given us their data, tell us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll update the date above and tell account holders by email or in the app before the change applies.

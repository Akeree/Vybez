Questions

Does Vybez stream my mixes?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

No. The room hears the music. Online, people see your tracklist and react. Each track links to the listener’s own streaming app.

Do I need to connect my DJ software?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

No. Export a history or playlist file from Serato, rekordbox, VirtualDJ or TRAKTOR and drop it on your Sets page. A live-sync helper is planned but not available yet.

What happens to my sets if I stop paying?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

They stay. You drop back to the free DJ plan, and every set and tracklist you added stays on your profile.

Can I pay in naira?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

Yes. Prices are set in naira. Other currencies may be offered at checkout once payments open.

Questions

{{#djsoon}}
Is Vybez Social free right now?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

Yes. Everything is free during early access. Payments aren’t switched on, and we’ll show you the price and ask before any night is ever charged.

What will a night cost later?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

The plan is ₦2,000 per night after your first free night, with no subscription. That price isn’t final; we’ll tell every host before it starts.

Do my guests need to pay or sign up?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

No. Guests open your link, RSVP with a name and get their pass. They never pay and never need an account.

Who can see my address?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

Only guests you approve. Everyone else with the link sees the area, not the full address.

{{/djsoon}} Does Vybez stream my mixes?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

No. The room hears the music. Online, people see your tracklist and react. Each track links to the listener’s own streaming app.

Do I need to connect my DJ software?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

No. Export a history or playlist file from Serato, rekordbox, VirtualDJ or TRAKTOR and drop it on your Sets page. A live-sync helper is planned but not available yet.

What happens to my sets if I stop paying?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

They stay. You drop back to the free DJ plan, and every set and tracklist you added stays on your profile.

Can I pay in naira?{{icon:chevron-right:18}}

Yes. Prices are set in naira. Other currencies may be offered at checkout once payments open.
